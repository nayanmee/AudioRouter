AudioRouter listening test

This page generates a quiet tone locally. Nothing is downloaded, recorded, or uploaded. Keep your output volume low initially.

Stopped

First open this file in one browser. Click its tone button, select an output in AudioRouter, and Start. Confirm the tone moves to that output without playing on the original output. Stop routing and confirm normal playback returns.

After verifying one browser, open this same file in the other browser, play the other tone, and choose a different output for it. Stop each route separately and test disconnecting one output.
